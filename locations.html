Our restaurants

Good to know

What changes between branches.

The kitchen and the menu are shared. These are the practical differences worth knowing before you choose.

Buffet sittings

Lunch & dinner

Weekday lunch and dinner buffet sittings currently run at Kempton Park. All three restaurants serve the breakfast buffet daily and the everyday buffet over weekends.

Family

Kids & games

Boksburg has the gaming area and the kiddies play area — pool, pinball and arcade machines — which makes it the easy pick for a long family lunch.

Seating

Inside & out

Kempton Park has an outside patio and a separate inside seating area. Edenvale sits right at Meadowdale Mall's entrance with covered outdoor tables.

The room

Room for the whole table.

Big tables, a proper bar, and enough space that nobody has to eat with their elbows in. Birthdays, work lunches, after-church Sundays — we've hosted all of it.
